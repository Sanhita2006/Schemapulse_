# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this community:

- Being accurate about what you measured, and about what you did not.
- Reviewing code on its merits and explaining the reasoning behind a change.
- Accepting that measurements beat opinions, and that a benchmark which does not
  reproduce on someone else's machine is not yet a benchmark.
- Assuming the other person is trying to help.

Behaviour that does not:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or review.
- Claiming a result you did not measure, or presenting fixture output as a live
  model result. That one is called out specifically because it is the failure
  mode this project exists to prevent; doing it in our own issues is
  disqualifying.
- Dismissing a safety or privacy concern as pedantic.

## Scope

This applies in project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commits — and
when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the maintainers by opening a confidential report
through the repository's private vulnerability reporting, or by contacting the
maintainers directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the maintainers.
